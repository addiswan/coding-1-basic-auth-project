=========================
APP PLANNING & DESIGN
=========================

1. PROJECT IDEA
-------------------------
- What is your app called? Quacker
- What type of app is it? (journal, listings, notes, etc.) Anonymous notes/posting app
- What will users use your app for? Miscellaneous things - they can say whatever they want with full anonymity

2. DATA MODEL (WHAT ARE YOU STORING?)
-------------------------
- What is a single “entry” in your app? A title and paragraph of content
- What fields will each entry have? (title, content, date, etc.) Title, content, username
- How will entries be connected to a user? Extra database criteria

3. CORE FEATURES (CRUD)
-------------------------
- What can users CREATE? Posts
- What will they see on the main page (READ)? Others' posts
- What can users EDIT (UPDATE)? Their own posts
- What can users DELETE? Their own posts

4. USER FLOW
-------------------------
- What happens after a user logs in? They'll see their dashboard with posts on it
- What pages/routes will your app need? (dashboard, create, edit, etc.) Dashboard, create, edit, delete, login, register
- Describe a typical user experience from login → using your app | Login, dashboard, create if they choose to post something

5. USER EXPERIENCE (UX)
-------------------------
- How should your app look and feel? Colorful/put together
- How will users navigate between pages? Buttons/links
- How will entries be displayed (list, cards, etc.)? A list of cards

6. OPTIONAL FEATURES
-------------------------
- What extra features might you add? (search, tags, sorting, etc.) A reply function (hopefully)
